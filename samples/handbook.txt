NOVACORE — FICTIONAL EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
This is fictional demonstration data, not employment or legal advice.

ANNUAL LEAVE
Employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave each calendar year. Up to 5 unused leave days may be carried forward to the next year. Leave requests must be submitted to the manager at least 7 days in advance.

REMOTE WORK
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days each week with manager approval. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Remote work outside the employee's registered country requires written approval.

EXPENSES
Meals during approved business travel may be reimbursed up to 1500 INR per day. Receipts are required for every reimbursement. Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.

SECURITY
Use multi-factor authentication for company accounts. Report suspected security incidents to the security team within 1 hour. Never share passwords or upload confidential data into unapproved tools.

LEARNING
Each employee has a yearly learning allowance of 10000 INR. Courses need manager approval before purchase. New employees complete onboarding within 14 days of joining.

The handbook does not identify a CEO, provide salary information, or specify a maternity leave policy. Do not infer these details.
